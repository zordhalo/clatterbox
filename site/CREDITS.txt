Clatterbox landing page — third-party audio credits
====================================================

The in-browser demo (site/app.js) uses a small subset of the same CC0 recordings
that ship in the app's "Classic Office" and "Tactile" builtin packs, plus two
fully synthesized packs ("Thock", "Click") generated at runtime with the Web
Audio API — no samples, no attribution needed for those two.
